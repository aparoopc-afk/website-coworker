Tickerly · In development · Built in public

The hardest trade is the one you don’t make.

Tickerly is a decision framework for active options traders. It thinks before you do. It says no more often than yes. And every Sunday at 6 PM, it grades itself against what actually happened — and quietly fixes what was wrong. Built against my own capital. Productizing now.

01 Why this exists

Retail options trading is a fair fight on the wrong terms. You have a charting app and your gut. The desk on the other side of your trade has a hundred screens, a calibration loop, and a written record of every time their thesis was wrong.

The asymmetry isn’t information — it’s discipline. Institutions don’t guess less. They reject more. They track what worked, retire what didn’t, and never trade their gut again. That’s the part you can’t buy on TradingView.

Most retail traders don’t lose because they pick the wrong direction. They lose because they took the trade at all.

Tickerly is built around that one observation. The product is not signals. The product is the rejections. The framework looks at a setup and tells you, with reasons: enter, defer, or skip. Most days, it says skip. That’s the feature.

03 Three principles
Principle 01
Discipline beats prediction.
Tickerly doesn’t guess where a stock is going. It measures whether the setup is the kind that has historically paid. The forecast is the byproduct, not the product. Most days, the most valuable output is the trade you didn’t take.
Principle 02
Rejection is the product.
A good framework rejects more trades than it accepts. Tickerly is calibrated so that on a typical day, four out of five candidates don’t clear the bar. That’s the part retail traders can’t replicate alone — the will to stand aside when the math doesn’t work.
Principle 03
Self-correction or death.
Every Sunday at 6 PM ET, the framework grades itself against the previous week’s outcomes. Patterns that drifted off get adjusted. Tiers that over-claimed get demoted. The framework that ships next week is not the one that shipped this week. That’s the loop. Without it, nothing else matters.
