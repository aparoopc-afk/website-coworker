01 The thesis

Most AI transformation work stalls because the architecture stops at the model. The engineering that separates a demo from a production system is the operational loop: how data flows in, how output is evaluated, how the system corrects itself, and how the humans running it stay in control.

Coworker Labs is built around that gap. We design the orchestration layer — agents, pipelines, calibration, guardrails, and the feedback loops that keep AI systems honest after deployment. Not slides. Running systems.
