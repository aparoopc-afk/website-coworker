I’m Aparoop. I help mid-market organizations build the operational loops that turn AI pilots into production systems. I also build them myself — Tickerly is the latest. I write The Loop weekly about what actually ships. Fractional engagements. Working systems. Accountable to outcomes.

01 The premise

70% of AI success or failure in enterprises is change management — not the model, not the vendor, not the tooling. The gap is muscle. The companies that win don’t pick the smartest tool. They pick the one their people actually use a year from now.

I help build that muscle in two directions. Top-down: mindset, governance, executive alignment, the courage to retire what isn’t working. Bottom-up: workflows, adoption, the operational loops that make new behaviors stick.

Two outcomes show up on the P&L:

→  Cost of services trending down (efficiency unlocked, not just demos shipped)
→  User adoption trending up (employees actually using the system, not just executives buying it)

Everything I do for clients — orchestration architecture, loop design — is in service of those two numbers.
