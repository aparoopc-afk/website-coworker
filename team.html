Twenty years of trading US and Indian equities — across cycles, asset classes, and personal drawdowns deep enough to remember exactly what bad tooling costs.

Two decades of building AI systems for enterprises, starting when the job title was still “data scientist with opinions.” coworker1 is where those two practices meet: the trading infrastructure I wished I’d had for the last twenty years, rebuilt with tools that only recently became practical for one person to use end-to-end.

Markets don’t care about team sizes. But the craft has shifted — models like Claude Opus 4.7, with 1M-token context, turn what used to be a team effort into a one-person workbench. I design the system; Claude reviews it, refactors it, catches what I miss. We call that arrangement coworkers.

Based in Pacific Time. Still trading. Still building.
